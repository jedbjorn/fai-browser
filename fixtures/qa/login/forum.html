Spring survey results: Estuary station 4

Posted by marta_v · 28 September 2026

Thanks to everyone who joined the spring count. Water temperature at the estuary station averaged 11.4 °C over the three survey mornings, about a degree warmer than last year.

Salinity readings stayed between 28 and 31 PSU. Full logs are attached to the station map.
