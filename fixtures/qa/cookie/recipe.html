The Best Brown Butter Cookie

Chewy in the middle, crisp at the edges. Makes 18 cookies.

Ingredients

  • 225 g unsalted butter, browned
  • 180 g dark brown sugar
  • 100 g caster sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 300 g plain flour
  • 200 g dark chocolate, chopped

Method

  1. Brown the butter and let it cool for 15 minutes.
  2. Beat in both sugars, then the eggs.
  3. Fold in the flour and chocolate.
  4. Bake at 175 °C for 11 minutes.
