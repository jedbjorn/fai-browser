Currency rounding question

You: When we convert an invoice from USD to EUR, should the rounding happen per line or on the total?
Assistant: Round each line to two decimals, then add the lines. Rounding only the total can leave the lines and the total a cent apart, which most auditors flag. If your ledger stores four decimals internally, keep them until the last step.
You: Thanks. Can you summarise that in one sentence for the team wiki?
Assistant: Convert and round every invoice line to two decimals first, then total the rounded lines.
